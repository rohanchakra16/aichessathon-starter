- Title: Non-linear king-danger term in the leaf evaluation
- Family: leaf-evaluation
- Hypothesis: Adding a cheap non-linear king-danger term to _model_evaluate reduces the recurring checkmate losses and converts some balanced-or-better shuffling draws into wins, without retraining. Inside the existing per-piece loop in _model_evaluate, OR every piece's board.attacks_mask(square) — including both kings — into one Python int attack-bitboard per colour. After the loop, for each side compute ring_bb = chess.BB_KING_ATTACKS[board.king(colour)] and d = chess.popcount(enemy_attack_bb & ring_bb), clamp d to a small cap, and add sign * -KS_WEIGHT * d * d to score (the sign loop already alternates the side-to-move perspective, so this simultaneously penalises danger around the mover's king and rewards pressure on the opponent's king). The quadratic shape gives alpha-beta a monotone, sharply rising reason to keep its own king shielded and to press an attack on an exposed enemy king that the flat castling scalar cannot express.
- Rationale: All six retained losses in exp-0089's own arena are checkmates, spanning both a middlegame king hunt with an uncastled king and several 50-70 move endgame king hunts, and the champion has literally no attacker-aware king term. The only prior king-safety attempt was a six-feature LINEAR trained residual that showed no offline signal and never reached the arena; a direct non-linear attacker-count penalty computed in agent.py is a materially different mechanism and is explicitly named as an open direction in the handoff. exp-0089 just demonstrated that a single cheap hand-scaled leaf term (mobility) can move the paired-arena score to 0.625 without touching the model, and reusing the attack masks already gathered in the mobility loop makes the added cost a handful of int-ORs and two popcounts per leaf. This is preferred over another progress-signal term (passed pawns) because it attacks the loss column directly rather than piling a second term onto the same sub-direction mobility already addressed.
- Guardrails: Reuse the existing piece loop only: accumulate attacks_mask into two Python ints (add the two kings, which the loop currently skips), with no second legal-move generation and no per-leaf object allocation beyond those two ints and the ring lookup. Use only documented python-chess API (board.king, board.attacks_mask or chess.BB_KING_ATTACKS, chess.popcount); never read library internals or any path outside agent.py and weights/. Clamp d at roughly 6 and fix KS_WEIGHT near 2-3 centipawns so the worst-case penalty stays around 0.8-1.1 pawn and only in genuine mating attacks, while a typical position stays well under a third of a pawn and far below the model's ~118 cp margin RMSE, keeping the trained PSQT and the accepted mobility term dominant.

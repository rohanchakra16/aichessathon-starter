- Title: Passed-pawn advancement term in the leaf evaluation
- Family: leaf-evaluation
- Hypothesis: Adding a cheap rank-scaled passed-pawn bonus to _model_evaluate gives alpha-beta a monotone progress gradient in simplified and locked positions that mobility cannot express, converting some of the balanced-or-better threefold-repetition draws into wins and improving conversion of favourable endgames without retraining. At import, precompute two 64-entry tuples of plain-int bitboards, _PASSED_MASK_WHITE/_PASSED_MASK_BLACK, where entry s is the union of the pawn's file and both adjacent files restricted to the ranks strictly ahead of s toward promotion (built from chess.BB_FILES / chess.square_rank / chess.square_file). In _model_evaluate, fetch the two pawn bitboards once via board.pieces_mask(chess.PAWN, chess.WHITE) and board.pieces_mask(chess.PAWN, chess.BLACK). Inside the existing `for colour, sign` / `for piece_type` loop, when piece_type == chess.PAWN and the current square's _PASSED_MASK[colour] has no intersection with the enemy pawn bitboard, add sign * PASSED_BONUS[relative_rank] to score, where relative_rank is the pawn's rank from its own side's perspective (0..6, 6 == 7th rank) and PASSED_BONUS is a fixed closed-form tuple such as (0, 8, 14, 24, 42, 74, 122) centipawns. Apply the bonus flat (no phase scaling, no connected/protected/blockaded sub-terms) in this first version so the mechanism is tested cleanly. The quadratic-ish rank schedule gives the search a sharply rising reason to push a passer and to trade into endgames where its passers matter, which is the specific progress signal missing from the shuffling draws.
- Rationale: The dominant retained failure mode across exp-0089 and exp-0090 is non-forced threefold-repetition draws from equal-or-better positions (29/64 in exp-0090), and multiple of those PGNs show the engine sitting on an extra passed pawn or a pawn majority in a rook ending and never advancing it. mobility (exp-0089, accepted) helped but is near-constant across moves in exactly those positions, so a second, orthogonal progress term is well motivated rather than redundant. Passed pawns are the canonical anti-draw progress heuristic, have never been tried in this project (absent from the registry and from both exhausted-mechanism lists), and are explicitly named as the next distinct term in the previous audit's own guardrails, which said that if the king-danger arena came back inconclusive the next step is passed pawns, then rook-on-open-file, then bishop pair. It is preferred over another king-attack term because exp-0090 just showed a static king-danger scalar does not move the needle, and preferred over rook-on-open-file / bishop pair because it targets the largest loss+draw column (endgame conversion) most directly. It is fully self-contained: precomputed masks plus a fixed coefficient tuple, no training pipeline, no new weights artifact, no change to how the trained model is used beyond an additive term far below its ~118-170 cp margin RMSE.
- Guardrails: Keep the trained PSQT model and the accepted mobility term dominant: PASSED_BONUS must stay small enough that a typical position contributes well under ~0.4 pawn and a realistic worst case (two passers on the 6th/7th) stays under ~1.2 pawn, i.e. below the model's margin RMSE; do not let any single-position passed-pawn contribution approach a minor piece. NPS/leaf-cost budget: precompute the mask tuples once at import (import has a 60 s budget; this is trivial) and store them as module-level constants — no per-leaf object allocation beyond the two board.pieces_mask int fetches, no second legal-move generation, no board.push. exp-0090's stress showed the worst single move at the 120 s clock was 1.09 s against the 2.25 s ceiling and 0.044 s against 0.75 s at the 1 s clock, so the added work (a handful of int-AND / popcount-free emptiness tests per pawn) must keep leaf cost within roughly 1.3x. Use only documented python-chess API (chess.BB_FILES, chess.BB_RANKS, chess.square_rank, chess.square_file, chess.square_mirror, board.pieces_mask); never read library internals and never touch any path outside agent.py and weights/. No change to _budget_seconds, the iterative-deepening loop, move ordering, quiescence, or the transposition table. Must pass the full stress_time_left_ms grid (1 / 25 / 100 / 1000 / 120000) with required_failures 0 and init under 50 s, and must hold model-ablation minimum_strength_score >= 0.625 with >= 8 divergent moves at the 3 s ablation clock. If the paired arena is inconclusive, do NOT re-tune PASSED_BONUS, add phase scaling, or bolt on connected/protected/blockaded/candidate-passer sub-terms and call it a new experiment — move to the next distinct positional term (rook-on-open-file, then bishop pair). If a later term genuinely needs an offline-trained artifact (a positional-feature retrain of weights/model.json), flag that for the human rather than expanding candidate permissions.
